MONUMENT GROWTH | ROOFING FIELD KIT
1 / INSPECTION TO BID
Record address, homeowner contact preference, decision maker and inspection date.
Record each facet’s measured surface area OR footprint plus pitch. Never apply pitch twice. Identify layers, penetrations, valleys, flashing, access, staging and disposal. Record photos and unresolved questions. Specify material, warranty source, exclusions, decking unit price and approval process for changes. Verify quantities and costs before issuing a binding bid.

2 / BID HANDOFF
Record bid date, scope version, assigned estimator, agreed next contact and decision status. Example: “Hi Ana, your Birch St proposal includes tear-off and disposal. Is there a scope item you’d like me to walk through?”
Second touch: “Should I keep this estimate open, revise the scope, or close it for now?” Stop on reply, booked, lost, withdrawn or opt-out.

3 / AFTER THE WORK
After final walkthrough: “Thanks for trusting us with your roof. Would you share an honest review of your experience? [your review link]”
After a completed repair and suitable weather: “Has the chimney stayed dry since our flashing repair? If anything looks off, reply and I’ll have the office call.”
Do not promise dispatch, diagnose a leak by text, or start another campaign after an opt-out.
